Click "Get Started", enter your email and a password, and you're in. No paperwork, no verification delays for basic features.

We accept Bitcoin, Ethereum, USDT (TRC20), Litecoin, and bank transfers. Deposits are credited to your wallet instantly after confirmation.

Most withdrawals are processed within 24 hours. Requests submitted before 6 PM are usually completed the same day.

We use bank-grade 256-bit encryption, cold storage for crypto assets, and multi-factor authentication on every account. Your funds are never held by a single custodian.
What is the minimum investment?
You can start with as little as $50 with our Starter plan. No maximum limits for the Classic plan.
How do I contact support?
Reach us any time via the live chat on this site, by email, or through our Telegram channel. Average response time is under 10 minutes.
